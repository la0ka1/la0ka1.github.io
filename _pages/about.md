<h2 class="section-heading">About Me</h2>

I am a third-year PhD student in [EECS at the University of Michigan](https://ece.engin.umich.edu/), advised by Prof. [Qing Qu](https://qingqu.engin.umich.edu/). Before this, I received my bachelor's degree from the [Department of Automation, Tsinghua University](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).
<!-- I have also collaborated with Prof. [Li Yi](https://ericyi.github.io/) and Prof. [Wenkai Lu](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1078/3204.htm). -->

<h2 class="section-heading">Research</h2>

My research focuses on first principles for deep neural networks, such as representations and low-dimensional structures. Recently, I have been interested in representation learning in diffusion models and multimodal models. 

**Representation learning would be the brain science for AI.**
