About Me
======
I am a second-year PhD student in [EECS at the University of Michigan](https://ece.engin.umich.edu/), advised by Prof. [Qing Qu](https://qingqu.engin.umich.edu/). Before this, I received my bachelor's degree from the [Department of Automation, Tsinghua University](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).
I have also collaborated with Prof. [Li Yi](https://ericyi.github.io/) and Prof. [Wenkai Lu](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1078/3204.htm).

Research
======
My research focuses on understanding and advancing deep neural networks through low-dimensional modeling and first-principles perspectives. Recently, I have been interested in diffusion models, particularly their (representation) learning and generalization abilities.
